Elle's Morning Hype

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Elle's Morning Hype is a study-motivation text message service for LSAT test-takers and law school students, operated by Veltson Bastien, a sole proprietor. This policy explains what information the service collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, provided when you opt in by texting START.
  • Text messages you send to the service, such as study questions or notes about your studying.
  • Basic delivery records, such as when a message was sent and whether it was delivered.

How we use it

  • To send you the study-motivation messages you signed up for.
  • To answer your questions and personalize messages using what you text us.
  • To respond to HELP and STOP requests and to keep the service working.

Service providers

To run the service, information is processed by service providers acting on our behalf: Twilio (message delivery), Anthropic (writing message text and answers), and ElevenLabs (voice features). They use this information only to provide these services.

SMS data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Retention

We keep message history only as long as needed to provide the service, and delete your information when you opt out or ask us to.

Contact

Questions about this policy: bastienveltson@outlook.com.
